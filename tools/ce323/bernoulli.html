Procedure & apparatus details

The duct converges through the 14° taper (taps h₁→h₅) then expands. Six manometers read static pressure head at taps of diameter 25.0, 13.9, 11.8, 10.7, 10.0 and 25.0 mm; the traversing probe reads total head htotal. For each run: set the FCV so h₁−h₅ hits the target (~150, 100, 50 mm), record the manometers, then — without touching the valve — time the collection of 4 L three times. After all three runs you can reverse the section to watch the steeper 21° taper converging (qualitative observation for your discussion).
